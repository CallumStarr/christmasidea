Ad · Secret Santa ideas for a £20 budget

By Callum Starr, NOAH Neurotech Ltd · Updated 7 November 2026

A £20 limit leaves room for something a little more useful than a novelty. Start with one thing you know about them, such as their commute, a hobby or a crowded desk. These are gift types to look for, not checked offers at today's prices. Include delivery in your £20 limit, and choose a different idea if the suitable options cost too much.

Ideas are selected from fixed gift-concept lists for the topic and budget, with practical buying checks. We have not bought or tested the products in these searches.

the options.

  • Use one clue you are confident about. Avoid jokes about someone's appearance, age or habits; a Secret Santa gift is usually opened in front of other people.
  • Spend on the gift rather than the wrapping. One good item is better than several small extras that take you over £20.
  • Black Friday listings can change quickly. Judge the delivered total against your £20 limit rather than the size of a claimed reduction, and check the returns window in case the gift needs exchanging after Christmas.

01

Insulated travel mug

For someone who takes a hot drink on the commute or school run.

What to look forCheck the capacity and whether it fits a car cup holder or bag pocket. A lid that comes apart for cleaning is more useful than a sealed one that is hard to wash.

Jigsaw puzzle

An evening project for someone who likes a quiet challenge.

What to look forMatch the piece count to puzzles they already finish and check the finished size against a table. An image with plenty of detail is easier than a large area of one colour.

Indoor herb growing kit

What to look forCheck what is in the box: pots, compost and seeds are not always all included. Herbs need a bright windowsill, so choose something else if their home has little light.
