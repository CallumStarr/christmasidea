Ad · Gift ideas for colleagues

By Callum Starr, NOAH Neurotech Ltd · Updated 7 November 2026

A gift for a colleague should be useful at work or easy to take home, without being too personal. Start with what you have noticed about their working day: the mug they always use, a crowded desk or a love of lists. These are gift types to look for within £15, not checked offers; include delivery in the total.

Ideas are selected from fixed gift-concept lists for the topic and budget, with practical buying checks. We have not bought or tested the products in these searches.

For the working day

the options.

  • Keep it neutral. Avoid jokes about their job, age or habits, and anything that might feel too personal when opened in front of other people.
  • Check allergies and dietary needs before buying anything edible. If you cannot ask, choose one of the non-food ideas below.
  • Black Friday listings can change quickly. Judge the delivered total against your £15 limit rather than the size of a claimed reduction, and allow for delivery before the last working day before Christmas.

Stoneware mug

What to look forCompare capacity and handle shape with the mug they use now. Check the care label for dishwasher and microwave guidance rather than assuming it from the photo.

Desk organiser

What to look forCheck the footprint against the space on their desk and how deep the compartments are. A colleague who hot-desks may prefer something that packs into a drawer or bag.

Compact folding umbrella

Adjustable phone stand
