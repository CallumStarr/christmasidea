Secret Santa ideas for a £10 budget

Start with one thing you know about them: the tea they bring to work, a desk full of plants or a fondness for puzzles. These are gift types to look for, not checked offers at today's prices. Include delivery in your £10 limit, and choose a different idea if the suitable options cost too much.

  • Use one thing you know about them, such as their tea break, desk plants or favourite kind of puzzle. Avoid personal jokes unless you are certain they will enjoy them.
  • Spend on the gift rather than elaborate packaging. One useful item is enough; there is no need to add extras that take you over the limit.
  • Check allergies before buying food, and sizes before buying socks. If you cannot ask, choose something that does not depend on either.

Small plant pot

Room for the next cutting on a plant lover's desk or windowsill.

What to look forChoose around the pot they want to put inside it: the internal width matters more than the outside measurement. A decorative cover pot needs an inner pot with drainage. Plants are not necessarily included.
