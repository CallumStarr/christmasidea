Gift ideas for tea and coffee lovers

Hot-drink lovers are often particular. Gear and accessories are safer than a bag of coffee or tea you have guessed at.

Explore the eight ideas ↓How to choose
The morning ritual

Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

  • Do you know how they brew? A pour-over fan and a cafetiere fan want different things.
  • A good mug or travel cup is a low-risk gift.
  • Avoid flavoured blends unless you know their tastes.

Pour-over coffee set

For the person who enjoys making the coffee almost as much as drinking it.

Insulated travel mug

A practical gift for a slow coffee drinker or a long morning commute.

What to look forCheck capacity, cup-holder fit and cleaning instructions. A splash-resistant lid is not necessarily safe to carry loose in a bag.

Tea infuser teapot

What to look forLook for a removable basket and check the serving capacity. Make sure the care instructions suit the way they wash their everyday crockery.

Hand coffee grinder

For someone ready to grind just enough beans for each brew.

What to look forCheck adjustment range and capacity against their brewing method. Espresso needs more precision than many entry-level grinders can offer.

A small upgrade to an everyday ritual, with no new technique to learn.

Milk frother

For a home hot chocolate or a milk-topped morning coffee.
