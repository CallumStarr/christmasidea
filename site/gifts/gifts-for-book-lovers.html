Gift ideas for book lovers

Keen readers often have a to-read pile already, so accessories and comforts are sometimes safer than guessing a title.

01

Clip-on reading light

For the person who means it when they say just one more chapter.

What to look forLook for adjustable brightness, a gentle low setting and a clip that will not damage pages. Check weight and the charging connection.

02

Leather or beaded bookmark

A useful little upgrade from the receipt currently keeping their place.

What to look forChoose something thin enough not to strain the spine. A specific colour or motif they like is more personal than a generic literary quote.

03

Book sleeve or cover

For the paperback that goes everywhere in the bottom of a bag.

What to look forMeasure a book they actually carry. Hardbacks, larger paperbacks and e-readers need different sizes; check the closure and washing instructions.

Search Amazon UK ↗
04

Reading cushion or lap desk

For longer reading sessions away from a desk.

What to look forCheck dimensions, support and storage space. Ask whether they prefer a cushion or a firm surface rather than choosing a bulky accessory unprompted.

Search Amazon UK ↗
05

Literary tote bag

Room for the library haul, in a design connected to a book they love.

What to look forCheck handle length, fabric weight and whether it has a useful inner pocket. Read the dimensions rather than relying on a styled photograph.

06

Bookends

For the growing stack that has started leaning into the houseplant.

What to look forLook for enough weight and a non-marking base. Check shelf depth and book height; very light decorative pairs may not hold heavy hardbacks.

07

Cosy blanket and tea set

A reading-evening kit assembled around their usual comforts.

What to look forChoose the tea they drink and a washable throw. Buying the two separately often gives you a better match than a pre-packed gift set.

08

Book tracker journal

For remembering the brilliant ending and deciding what to read next.

What to look forCheck the page layout and number of entries. Some readers want prompts and ratings; others would rather have space for their own notes.
