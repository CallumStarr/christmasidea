Gift ideas for history lovers

History lovers are often specific about periods and places. Start with the era they love, then pick something to read, build or display.

Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

  • Check what they already own; keen readers often have the famous titles.
  • Pick one period rather than a general 'history' gift.
  • Maps, prints and models make good display gifts.

Narrative history book

An absorbing account of the period they always bring up at dinner.

What to look forStart with their favourite era, then check their shelves. Read a sample: a specialist reader and a casual enthusiast may want very different levels of detail.

02

Historical atlas

For someone who likes to understand where a story happened, not just when.

What to look forCheck the geographic focus, period covered and printed size. Clear maps with readable labels matter more than an impressive page count.

03

Roman or medieval model kit

A weekend project with a finished object for the shelf.

What to look forMatch the historical period and skill level. Check the scale, display dimensions and whether paints, glue or tools are needed separately.

04

History trivia game

A chance to put all those accumulated facts to use with friends.

What to look forCheck player numbers, typical playing time and geographic bias. A very specialist quiz can leave everyone except the recipient out of the game.

Search Amazon UK ↗
05

Reproduction map print

A particular city, journey or home town made into something to keep.

What to look forCheck the date, print dimensions and whether a frame is included. Make sure a reproduction is clearly described as such.

06

Museum-inspired puzzle

An afternoon with a favourite collection, without finding room for another ornament.

What to look forChoose a subject they recognise and a suitable piece count. Check the finished dimensions before committing their dining table to it.

Search Amazon UK ↗
07

Timeline wall chart

For someone who enjoys connecting people and events across different periods.

What to look forRead the period coverage and actual dimensions. Fold-out formats suit limited wall space; tiny text can make a large timeline frustrating.

08

Biography of someone they admire

A specific choice that shows you remembered the person behind their fascination.

What to look forCheck the author and whether it is a new account or a reissue. For a serious enthusiast, a well-sourced biography is preferable to a general gift edition.
