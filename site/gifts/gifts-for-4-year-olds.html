Gift ideas for 4-year-olds

Four-year-olds are curious, imaginative and increasingly independent. The best gifts give them something to pretend, build, sort or make, rather than something that only does one thing when a button is pressed.

Explore the eight ideas ↓How to choose
Play & making

Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

  • Check the age label on the box. If the child has younger siblings, keep small-part toys well away from them.
  • If the family prefers a quiet house, look for open-ended play such as dressing up, building and drawing instead of toys with sound effects.
  • Pick one thing they can do with a grown-up and one they can do alone.
