Gift ideas for mum

Think about what your mum looks forward to doing: reading a new novel, trying a recipe or getting stuck into a puzzle. A title she has mentioned or a photograph she wants to print gives you somewhere to start. Choose around her interests, rather than a label saying 'for mum'.

Before you choose

Narrow down
the options.

  • Listen for a title, hobby or worn-out favourite she mentions. Specific clues are more useful than choosing something simply because it is labelled 'for mum'.
  • For a personalised gift, check the spelling, photograph and order deadline yourself. Allow time to approve a proof rather than rushing the final step.
  • Keep a receipt for clothing or comfort items. For scented products, ask what she uses rather than guessing from the packaging.

01

Sofa throw

A blanket for her favourite reading or television spot, in a colour she would choose herself.

What to look forChoose a size she can get under, not just drape over the sofa arm. A washable fabric suits everyday use; a delicate decorative throw may need more care than she wants.

02

Personalised photo book

Bring together photographs from a holiday, family occasion or another time she would enjoy looking back on.

What to look forUse the original image files where possible and check every crop, caption and spelling. Leave time to review a proof, and read the seller's rules on corrections and returns for personalised orders.

03

Bath salts

Something to add to a bath she already enjoys taking, rather than a new routine to adopt.

What to look forUse a familiar product as your guide to fragrance and ingredients. Read the use instructions, and choose a different gift if scented bath products are not something she likes.

Tea and mug gift set

Her preferred tea and a mug she can wrap her hands around.

What to look forThe tea matters more than the box. If no set has her usual blend and a mug of the right size, buy them separately. Read ingredients and allergen information for any added treats.

Book by a favourite author

A new title from a writer she already enjoys, rather than a guess based on a bestseller list.

What to look forCheck her shelves, e-reader or wish list before ordering. Match her preferred format and print size, and confirm the exact title and edition rather than buying by cover alone.

Cookbook in a cuisine she enjoys

New recipes to explore if cooking is something she does for pleasure.

What to look forBrowse a few recipes: would she enjoy making them, and can she get the ingredients? Match her dietary preferences and available time, rather than choosing a book for the meals you hope she will cook.

Slippers

A replacement for a worn pair, in the style she already finds comfortable.

What to look forAsk about open or closed backs, size and sole preference. Check the size chart and return policy, and keep the receipt: padding and fit are difficult to judge from a photograph.

Jigsaw puzzle
