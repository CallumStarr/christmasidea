Gift ideas for mum

The best gift for mum reflects something she loves or something she never buys for herself. A thoughtful small gift often beats an expensive generic one.

Explore the eight ideas ↓How to choose
Her kind of Christmas

Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

  • Think about what she does in her free time and give something that makes it nicer.
  • A personal touch, like a photo or a handwritten note, matters more than the price.
  • Skip fragrance unless you know the scents she wears.

Soft throw blanket

For her usual spot on the sofa, in a colour that actually belongs in her home.

Bath-time treats

For someone who already enjoys taking a little time over a bath.

Good-quality mug and treats

Her usual tea or coffee, with something good to go alongside it.

What to look forChoose the drink she actually buys, and check dietary needs. One well-chosen mug and her favourite biscuits can beat an oversized generic hamper.

Book by her favourite author

An easy next chapter when you know whose books she keeps returning to.

Cookbook for her cuisine
