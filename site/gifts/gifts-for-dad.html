Gift ideas for dad

Dads are famously hard to buy for. Pick a hobby he talks about, then give something that makes it easier, tidier or more enjoyable.

Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

  • Listen for complaints; the thing he grumbles about is a gift opportunity.
  • Avoid gadgets he has not asked for; they get returned.
  • Experience gifts and good food are reliable.

Compact tool organiser

For the person who owns the right bit somewhere, but can never find it.

What to look forCheck the tools or small parts he wants to store and measure the available space. Useful compartments matter more than a very large case.

02

Insulated flask

A proper hot drink at the end of a walk or halfway through a morning in the garden.

What to look forCheck capacity, empty weight and how easily it opens and cleans. A cup lid is useful outdoors; a large flask can be too bulky for a small bag.

03

Biography or history book

A quiet evening with the subject he always stops to watch on television.

What to look forFind out which period or person interests him, then check what he owns. A specific choice beats the latest general bestseller.

04

Cordless torch or head torch

For the dark corner of the shed, an evening walk or a camping trip.

What to look forCheck weight, charging connection and the low-light setting as well as maximum brightness. An adjustable strap and simple controls are worth looking for.

05

Craft beer or whisky tasting glasses

For someone who already enjoys comparing a couple of favourites.

What to look forCheck the drink he actually likes and how the glasses should be washed. Skip alcohol-related gifts if you are not sure they would be welcome.

06

Wallet or card holder

A simple replacement for the one that has quietly started falling apart.

What to look forCheck whether he carries notes, coins or just cards. Match his preferred layout rather than assuming smaller is automatically better.

07

Board game for two

A gift that includes a reason to sit down and spend an evening together.

What to look forCheck that it is good with exactly two players, not merely playable with two. Match the rules complexity and playing time to his taste.

08

Bluetooth speaker

For music in the workshop or something to listen to while potting plants.

What to look forCheck battery life, charging connection and the stated water-resistance rating. Find out whether he already has a speaker system he likes.
