Ad · Gift ideas for a partner

By Callum Starr, NOAH Neurotech Ltd · Updated 7 November 2026

You probably know your partner's habits better than anyone: how they spend a Sunday, what they reach for every morning and what has worn out. Use that knowledge rather than a gift labelled 'for him' or 'for her'. These are gift types to look for within £50, not checked offers; include delivery in the total.

Ideas are selected from fixed gift-concept lists for the topic and budget, with practical buying checks. We have not bought or tested the products in these searches.

Just for them

Narrow down
the options.

  • Think about something they have mentioned wanting, borrowing or replacing this year. A specific clue is more useful than a generic romantic gift.
  • For anything sized or worn, check the size and style of a current favourite first. Keep the receipt, as fit is hard to judge from a photograph.
  • Black Friday listings can change quickly. Judge the delivered total against your £50 limit rather than the size of a claimed reduction, and allow extra time for anything made to order.

Canvas weekend bag

For short trips away together or visits to family.

What to look forCheck the dimensions against luggage racks or the car boot, and look at the strap and zip. An extra inside pocket is useful, but more compartments add weight.

Board game for two

A game to play together on winter evenings.

What to look forCheck that it plays well with two, not only that two is the minimum on the box. Choose a playing time that fits a weeknight and a theme you would both enjoy.

Wool scarf

A warm scarf in a colour they would choose themselves.

What to look forLook at the scarves they already wear for length and colour. Check the fibre content and washing instructions; some wool needs hand-washing.

Custom map print

A print of a place you share, such as a city, a favourite walk or a holiday spot.

What to look forCheck the print size and whether a frame is included. Confirm the place name and spelling on any proof, and allow time for made-to-order dispatch.

Slim card holder

A slim replacement for a worn wallet, if they mostly pay by card.

What to look forCount the cards they carry every day and compare the number of slots. Check the size against their current wallet and keep the receipt in case they prefer the old layout.

Picnic blanket

For walks, parks and summer plans to look forward to.

What to look forCheck the unfolded size and whether it folds into a carry strap. A waterproof backing helps on damp grass but can make the blanket harder to wash.

Wireless earbuds

For music or calls on the commute or a run.
