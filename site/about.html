About Santa's Helper

Santa's Helper helps when you know a person better than you know what to buy them. Describe what they enjoy, add your budget and mention anything they already own or would rather not receive.

What the gift finder does

The finder uses AI to suggest gift ideas from the details you share. If the AI service is unavailable, it may offer ideas from a fixed catalogue instead; the results tell you which was used. You can save ideas to a shortlist in your browser and set aside the ones that do not fit.

The gift guides are separate, fixed lists. They explain who an idea might suit and what to check, such as sizes, compatibility, materials and extra costs. They do not change to match a description you enter.

What to check before buying

We have not bought or tested the products in these searches. Shopping links open an Amazon UK or eBay UK search unless labelled as a matched listing. Prices, stock and delivery costs can change, and the suggestions do not guarantee that a suitable option is available within your budget.

If product matching is available, a “Matched Amazon listing” or “Matched eBay listing” label applies only to that retailer's offer. An attributed eBay example photo illustrates the gift idea without linking to either shop; choose Amazon or eBay using the equally prominent buttons below. The Amazon product may differ. When available, Amazon listing photos take priority and link to their Amazon listing. Matching is not a review or an endorsement. Check the seller, contents, compatibility, age guidance and total cost yourself. For children's gifts, follow the product's safety instructions and ask a parent if you are unsure.

The guide images are AI-created illustrations, not photographs of products we have tested.
