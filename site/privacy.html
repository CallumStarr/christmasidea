Privacy notice

Last updated 6 November 2026.

This notice explains how Santa's Helper handles the information you share. Please leave names, addresses, health information and other sensitive details out of gift requests.

The service is for adults aged 18 and over shopping for others, including children. Children should not submit requests. Please describe a recipient's interests without identifying them. See our Terms of Use.

Our purposes and lawful bases

We rely on legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) for the following purposes. We balance these interests against your rights and limit the information used:

  • Providing the finder, AI suggestions, reviewed-library suggestions, caching and optional retailer searches: our interest is in answering the gift request you choose to submit.
  • Hosting, service logs, rate limits and Turnstile bot checks: our interest is in keeping the service available and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Aggregate gift feedback, review summaries and Cloudflare Web Analytics: our interest is in understanding service usage and improving gift suggestions without building visitor profiles. IP-derived rate limits are pseudonymous, not anonymous.
  • Support, privacy correspondence and business enquiries: our interest is in answering messages and resolving problems. Where we must keep records or respond to statutory rights requests, the basis is legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Locally saved wishlists, recently viewed ideas, sharing and reminders: our interest is in providing the device features you request. List names and birthday dates are not uploaded to our service.

Right to object: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time, including on grounds relating to your particular situation. Email us using the details above. We will stop unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or need the information for legal claims. You can use the fixed guides without submitting a finder request.

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, portability. We normally respond within one month. We cannot identify an individual from aggregate counters alone and may need enough information to locate any correspondence or cached request.

Right to complain: you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority. You do not have to contact us first.

What we use, and why

iPhone and Android app: this notice also covers the Santa's Helper app. The app uses the same recommendation service and sends the same descriptions, questionnaire answers and limited feedback described below. Wishlists are stored locally inside the app on your device, not synced to an account; removing the app may remove them. Sharing or copying a list uses your device's share sheet or clipboard only when you choose that action. Shopping pages open in a browser. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera or location. Its security check loads a Santa's Helper page and Cloudflare Turnstile over the internet, so those services receive connection information. New recommendations require an internet connection.

  • Gift requests: when a fresh, reviewed gift library can supply enough suitable ideas for your details, suggestions can be selected without a new AI call. Otherwise, the description or questionnaire answers you submit are sent to Anthropic's AI service to generate suggestions. This can include titles of previously shown ideas when you ask for a different set, so they are not suggested again. Anthropic is based in the United States. Please describe interests and preferences without identifying the recipient.
  • Rate limits and abuse prevention: the Worker receives the connection IP address. Santa's Helper uses a salted, daily-changing hash for rate-limit counters rather than intentionally storing the raw address in its own rate-limit state. Cloudflare processes network and request data to host and secure the service.
  • Optional product discovery: if a retailer's integration is enabled, we send gift-concept search terms, rather than your original recipient description or explanation, to Amazon's Creators API or eBay's Browse API to look for products. These terms may reflect interests and preferences in your request. eBay listing details are processed for the current response only, not saved to our server caches or database; we do not retain eBay seller or account data. If Amazon discovery is enabled, its product details and image URLs may be cached for up to one hour. If a product image is shown, your browser loads it directly from that retailer's image servers, which receive your connection information; Santa's Helper does not store the image file.
  • Repeated requests: as you swipe through ideas, the finder can preload another batch when three cards remain. This resubmits your recipient details with up to 24 recent idea titles to avoid repeats. Automatic loading pauses after an error, an empty batch or catalogue fallback; you can request more manually. Generated results may be cached by Cloudflare for up to 24 hours (one hour for a short result) so identical requests do not need another AI call.
  • Shortlist and wishlists: wishlist names, saved idea names, search terms and categories are kept in local storage in your browser on this device. You can create separate named lists; your original shortlist is retained as the first list. List names are not sent to our AI provider, feedback service or telemetry, and are not included in shared links. The shortlist itself is not uploaded to Santa's Helper. Saving an idea can send a limited feedback event, described below. If browser storage is unavailable, the list lasts only for the current visit.
  • Gift-finder feedback: the browser may report that ideas were shown, clicked, saved or dismissed, that you requested different ideas, or your explicit good/poor-match ratings and self-reported purchases. It sends controlled labels: strategy, source (model, graph or catalogue), discovery flag, list position, gift category, one recognised interest topic, broad age and budget bands, and ranking-policy version. Current clients also send a signed receipt containing those controlled labels, a separate random card identifier and an expiry time. It contains no recipient description, generated idea text, IP address or user/session identifier. Receipts are valid for 24 hours. To prevent repeat counting, the server temporarily stores only the random card identifier, event slot and expiry; expired markers are deleted during normal service activity or the next daily cleanup. Good and poor-match ratings share one slot per receipt. Aggregate counts remain stored per day for 90 days. Signed receipts verify that a card was issued, not that a unique person saw it; display position and purchases remain self-reported. The operator can export aggregate totals for analysis. Once enough feedback and quality checks support a change, a reviewed, versioned policy may slightly reorder already-validated ideas for similar requests. Clicks alone do not count as good recommendations, and purchases reported here are not retailer-verified. Feedback does not automatically retrain the AI. Dismissed ideas are excluded from subsequent batches within the current search, using temporary browser memory.
  • Reviewed gift library: when an idea maps confidently to our controlled gift vocabulary, feedback can also include that vocabulary's concept key and version. These are fixed generic gift types, not newly stored descriptions of people or arbitrary generated titles. Concept counters use the same daily aggregation and 90-day retention as other finder feedback. Only signed, replay-deduplicated reports from requests with one recognised topic and no recognised traits contribute evidence for reuse approval; older clients' unsigned reports remain limited to usage/category feedback. Recent explicit ratings can inform a human-reviewed library for similar broad interests, age and budget bands. Multi-interest, unfamiliar or nuanced requests still use the ordinary recommendation service. Library policies expire and do not automatically approve themselves; feedback on library results and their replacement cards is kept separate from the control evidence used for approval. The operator can export verified concept totals through an authenticated endpoint. No new personal-description archive or per-user recommendation history is created.
  • Website feedback: the optional rating and note are composed in your email app. Santa's Helper does not receive or store them unless you choose to send the email; if you do, it arrives as ordinary support correspondence. Please do not include personal or sensitive details.
  • Feedback review notifications: after 200 additional shown cards across the site, even without ratings, or once a cohort has enough explicit feedback, the operator may publish an aggregate summary in a GitHub issue to prompt human analysis. It contains site-wide interaction totals or broad interest, age, budget and gift-category labels with totals, not individual requests, gift titles or identifying details. These summaries have the repository's visibility and may be public. Shown-card volume alone is not evidence of recommendation quality, and notifications do not automatically change the engine.
  • Feedback rate limits: a daily-changing value derived from the connection IP limits how much feedback a visitor can send per minute and per day. It is not included in the aggregate feedback counters or receipt markers, and the raw IP is not stored in those counters. This is pseudonymous processing, not a guarantee of anonymity.
  • Shopping and affiliate links: when you follow an Amazon or eBay link, that retailer receives the request and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy notice. Approved affiliate links include a campaign or tracking ID identifying Santa's Helper, not the recipient.

Cookies and site analytics

Saved and shared gift lists: you can keep up to 20 named wishlists with up to 50 gift concepts each on this device. List names stay local and are excluded from shared links; please avoid sensitive details in names or saved ideas. Recently viewed gift concepts are also kept only in this browser, capped at 12, and can be cleared on the homepage. The site does not upload these lists. Shared links encode a copy in the URL fragment after #; that fragment is not sent in the HTTP request to our server. Anyone receiving the link can read or forward the list, and deleting a local entry does not revoke shared copies. Shared lists exclude your description, list name, birthday date and retailer listing data. Active partner destinations are reconstructed from our current inventory and affiliate settings.

Sharing and reminders: native sharing, clipboard copying and WhatsApp sharing run in the browser. Choosing WhatsApp sends your chosen gift text and links to WhatsApp under its own privacy terms. Calendar reminders are downloaded as .ics files; birthday dates are used temporarily in the browser, not saved by the website. Importing a reminder into a calendar app is subject to that app's handling of information.

Optional monetization measurements and ads: external monetization analytics and ad scripts are disabled by default. The local event hook exposes only controlled event, network, gift-category and recipient labels, without names, email addresses, descriptions, list contents or identifiers. Enabling a Plausible, Umami or contextual-ad integration requires an operator review of provider logging, retention and consent requirements; cookieless does not mean that no personal data is processed. This website does not claim zero GDPR liability. Sponsor enquiries use your email app and become ordinary business correspondence if you send them, not a website marketing database.

Santa's Helper does not set advertising cookies. The shortlist uses browser local storage rather than a cookie. Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled and measures aggregate page views without cookies or fingerprinting. Cloudflare Turnstile checks requests to help prevent automated abuse. Cloudflare also processes network and request data to host and secure the service. Amazon's or eBay's own privacy and cookie notices apply after you follow their links.

How long we keep information, and your choices

Our server caches AI results for up to 24 hours, or one hour for a short result. eBay listing data is not persisted; it is processed for the current response and displayed temporarily in your browser. Amazon product details and image URLs may be cached for up to one hour if enabled; retailer image files are not stored. The shortlist stores our gift-concept names and queries, not the matched eBay listing data. A daily scheduled cleanup prunes feedback aggregate counters once they are 90 days old.

Undelivered aggregate review summaries are also pruned after 90 days. Small site-wide and per-cohort notification cutoff records are retained to prevent repeat alerts; they are not user histories. Published GitHub summaries and operator CSV exports are separate copies, not removed by server counter cleanup.

Service-usage and rate-limit counters use daily and minute buckets; product discovery also uses hourly and second buckets. Old buckets are removed during scheduled cleanup. The IP-derived value used for per-visitor limits changes daily. These are separate from the 90-day feedback totals. Cache and counter limits describe Santa's Helper's storage, not the providers' own retention periods.
