What we use, and why

  • Gift requests: the description or questionnaire answers you submit are sent to Anthropic's AI service to generate suggestions. This can include titles of previously shown ideas when you ask for a different set, so they are not suggested again. Anthropic is based in the United States. Please describe interests and preferences without identifying the recipient.
  • Rate limits and abuse prevention: the Worker receives the connection IP address. Santa's Helper uses a salted, daily-changing hash for rate-limit counters rather than intentionally storing the raw address in its own rate-limit state. Cloudflare processes network and request data to host and secure the service.
  • Optional product discovery: if a retailer's integration is enabled, we send gift-concept search terms, rather than your original recipient description or explanation, to Amazon's Creators API or eBay's Browse API to look for products. These terms may reflect interests and preferences in your request. Product details and image URLs may be cached by our server for up to one hour. If a product image is shown, your browser loads it directly from that retailer's image servers, which receive your connection information; Santa's Helper does not store the image file.
  • Repeated requests: generated results may be cached by Cloudflare for up to 24 hours (one hour for a short result) so identical requests do not need another AI call.
  • Shortlist: saved idea names, search terms and categories are kept in local storage in your browser on this device. The shortlist itself is not uploaded to Santa's Helper. Saving an idea can send a limited feedback event, described below. If browser storage is unavailable, the list lasts only for the current visit.
  • Gift-finder feedback: the browser may report that ideas were shown, clicked, saved or dismissed, or that you requested different ideas. For each idea, it sends only the strategy, source (model, graph or catalogue), discovery flag and position in the list. The feedback endpoint does not receive your recipient description, generated idea text or a session identifier. It stores daily aggregate counters, not a record of each event. These help evaluate the finder and do not change live recommendations.
  • Feedback rate limits: a daily-changing value derived from the connection IP limits how much feedback a visitor can send per minute. It is not included in the aggregate feedback counters, and the raw IP is not stored in those counters. This is pseudonymous processing, not a guarantee of anonymity.
  • Shopping and affiliate links: when you follow an Amazon or eBay link, that retailer receives the request and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy notice. Approved affiliate links include a campaign or tracking ID identifying Santa's Helper, not the recipient.

Cookies and site analytics

Santa's Helper does not set advertising cookies. The shortlist uses browser local storage rather than a cookie. If enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate page views without cookies or fingerprinting, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks requests to help prevent automated abuse. Cloudflare also processes network and request data to host and secure the service. Amazon's or eBay's own privacy and cookie notices apply after you follow their links.

International processing

Using the finder involves sending your request to Anthropic, which is based in the United States. Cloudflare, Amazon and eBay may also process information outside the United Kingdom. Their notices below explain their handling of information; this page does not mean your request stays in the UK. For questions about these providers or international processing, contact the site operator using the details above.

How long we keep information, and your choices

Our server caches AI results for up to 24 hours, or one hour for a short result. If product discovery is enabled, product details and image URLs are cached for up to one hour; retailer image files are not stored. A daily scheduled cleanup prunes feedback aggregate counters once they are 90 days old.

Service-usage and rate-limit counters use daily and minute buckets; product discovery also uses hourly and second buckets. Old buckets are removed during scheduled cleanup. The IP-derived value used for per-visitor limits changes daily. These are separate from the 90-day feedback totals. Cache and counter limits describe Santa's Helper's storage, not the providers' own retention periods.

You can browse the fixed gift guides without submitting a gift request to the AI service. Clear a shortlist by removing its entries or clearing this site's browser storage. For questions about your information, including access or deletion requests, use the contact details below.

For privacy questions or requests about your information, email callumstarrwork@gmail.com or use the details on the About and contact page.
